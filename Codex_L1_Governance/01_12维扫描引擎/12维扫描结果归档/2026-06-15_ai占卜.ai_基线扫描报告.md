### Dimension 6 - Feedback Loop

The loop exists as documents, but it is not yet measurable as an automated cycle. Add a required "feedback update" section to every project review packet and track whether a template, skill, or script changed.

### Dimension 10 - Automation

Best first automations:

- evidence row completeness checker
- gate decision JSON refresher
- secret-shape scan wrapper
- 12D scorecard generator

### Dimension 11 - Capability Boundary

Boundary handling is strong: `plan-only` does not become execution approval, and missing evidence stays missing. The weak point is that proposed skills are not yet verified executable Codex skills.
