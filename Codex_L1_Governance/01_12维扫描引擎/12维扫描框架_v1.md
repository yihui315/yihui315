# 12维扫描框架 v1

## Purpose

The 12D scan is a shared diagnostic framework for Codex-managed projects. It measures governance maturity, workflow efficiency, evidence readiness, and system-level reuse potential.

## Scoring Scale

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0-2 | Weak: absent, ad hoc, or repeatedly failing |
| 3-5 | Moderate: partially present but not reliable |
| 6-8 | Strong: usable and repeatable with known gaps |
| 9-10 | Excellent: validated, reusable, and low-risk |

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What to inspect | Common blocker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 重复工作流识别 | Repeated manual tasks, recurring validation steps, repeated file updates | No inventory of repeatable operations |
| 2 | Skill触发与匹配失败 | Whether the right Codex skill/tool is selected from context | Skills are implicit or unknown |
| 3 | Sub-Agent角色与协作断点 | Role clarity, handoff quality, review ownership | Agents overlap or duplicate work |
| 4 | Worker并行执行效率 | Safe parallel work lanes and merge strategy | Serial execution for independent checks |
| 5 | 输出质量与厚度评估 | Completeness, specificity, and usefulness of outputs | Thin reports or vague next steps |
| 6 | 反馈闭环与Loop缺失 | Whether review findings feed back into templates and checks | Notes exist but do not change behavior |
| 7 | 失败案例与错误沉淀 | Structured capture of failed attempts and constraints | Failures are buried in chat history |
| 8 | 项目/知识地图完整性 | Navigation across docs, gates, assets, and evidence | Artifacts exist but are hard to locate |
| 9 | 业务价值与优先级排序 | Blocker priority, value impact, and scope control | Low-value work precedes gate blockers |
| 10 | 自动化机会 | Scripts, validators, scheduled checks, and reusable prompts | Manual governance refreshes |
| 11 | 能力边界与官方机制适配 | Alignment with Codex skills, plugins, MCPs, and safe execution limits | Unclear tool boundary or unsupported claims |
| 12 | 整体系统优化与评分 | Health score, trend, cross-project reuse | No global baseline or maturity trend |

## Required Output

Each scan must include:

- project name and date
- data sources used
- per-dimension score and rationale
- top 3 blockers
- top 3 reuse opportunities
- recommended next action
- evidence limitations
- whether the scan changes any gate state

## Gate Interaction

A 12D scan can recommend gate changes, but it cannot pass a gate by itself. Gate changes require the relevant gate template and evidence standard.
